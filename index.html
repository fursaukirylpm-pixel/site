About

Compliance is a product. So I treat it like one.

I started in back-office and AML operations, learned where the manual work and risk really live, and grew into the Product Owner who rebuilds those systems from the ground up.

At a 1,000-person regulated broker I owned an internal compliance & operations platform used by 400+ agents across 7 jurisdictions — the full financial-crime lifecycle: onboarding, KYC/AML, sanctions & PEP screening, payments, risk and anti-fraud. I built the team that builds it, pitched the roadmap to C-level, and prioritised every bet on ROI.

My north star was simple: get more clients through the funnel cleanly, without a human ever touching their case. I took that from 35% to 85%.
