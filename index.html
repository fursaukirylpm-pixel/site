About

Onboarding and compliance are products. So I treat them like one.

I started in back-office and AML operations, learned where the manual work and risk really live, and grew into the Product Manager who rebuilds those systems from the ground up.

At a 1,000-person regulated broker I owned an internal compliance & operations platform used by 400+ agents across 7 jurisdictions — the full financial-crime lifecycle: onboarding, KYC/AML, sanctions & PEP screening, payments, risk and anti-fraud. I built the team that builds it, pitched the roadmap to C-level, and prioritised every bet on ROI.

My north star is simple: get more clients through the funnel cleanly, without a human ever touching their case. I took that from 35% to 85%.

Today at Pepperstone I own client onboarding across web and mobile. I wrote the FY26/27 strategy and 3-year roadmap, sequence license launches across regulators, and move markets off the legacy monolith — while shipping code myself across repos, so the roadmap turns into production fast.
